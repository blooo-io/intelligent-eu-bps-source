# REQ-002

The system shall compute, for each billing period, the amount owed between each consumer and each producer from the settled trades between them. Trade prices are taken as EUR; for communities whose currency is not EUR (e.g. CHF), amounts are converted at the ECB reference rate of the last business day of the period (or of the settlement date for an early settlement), and each invoice is issued in a single currency, the community currency, showing the rate and its date.
