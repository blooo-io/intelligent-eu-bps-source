# REQ-004

The system shall include in each invoice the grid-fee, levy and tax components derived from the tariff data published for the community on the EWDS by the DSO / grid operator (ontology Tariff class), as soon as such data are available; until then the grid-fee amount is 0 and the invoice states that grid fees are billed by the supplier or DSO.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6 (2026-11-24) source alignment: reinstated: the ontology defines an Invoice as derived from trades AND tariffs, with energy / grid-fee / tax amounts (D3.1 §4; ontology Invoice), and the D5.5 scope lists the DSO tariffs and flexibility fees (ERE, D4.5 due M30) as billing inputs. Target D5.10 because D4.5 is due M30; V1 keeps grid fee = 0.
