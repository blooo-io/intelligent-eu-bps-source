# REQ-007

The system shall expose to both parties of each invoice the list of the trades it covers (delivery period, kWh, price per kWh, amount), the energy amount, the VAT (rate configurable per community, 0 by default) and the total. Grid fees, taxes and levies of the supplier or DSO are not part of P2P invoices; on published records the grid-fee amount is 0.
