# REQ-007

The system shall show on each invoice the producer (issuer), the consumer (addressee), the trades covered (delivery period, kWh, price per kWh, amount), the energy amount, the grid-fee amount, the tax amount (VAT rate configurable per community, 0 by default) and the total. The grid-fee amount is 0 until tariff data are available (REQ-004).

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: legal invoice per pair; amount split energy / grid fee / tax as in the ontology Invoice class.
