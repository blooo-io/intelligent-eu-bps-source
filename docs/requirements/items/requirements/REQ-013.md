# REQ-013

The system shall support exporting or confirming deletion of a participant's personal data upon a valid GDPR data-subject request received through the portal or the community manager, and shall propagate erasure to the payment provider and the identity provider using the stored external identifiers; invoices and accounting records are kept for the legal retention period.
