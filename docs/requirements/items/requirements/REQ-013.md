# REQ-013

The system shall support exporting or confirming deletion of a participant's personal data upon a valid GDPR data-subject request received from the EWDS (issued by the pilot data controller), through the portal or from the community manager, and shall propagate erasure to the payment provider and the identity provider using the stored external identifiers; invoices and accounting records are kept for the legal retention period.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6 (2026-11-24) source alignment: erasure requests originating from the EWDS added (D5.5 scope hypothesis H6; D3.1 pilot managers are controllers).
>
> INT-463 (2026-11-26): IdP account erasure is owned by the project UI (R2M); BPS records outcome `delegated_to_project_ui` and does not call Keycloak Admin delete. R2M must erase the login account on DSAR.
