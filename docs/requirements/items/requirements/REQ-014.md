# REQ-014

The system shall let a consumer pay each invoice addressed to them directly to the producer's payment-provider connected account (direct charge, card or SEPA Direct Debit), so that the platform never holds the funds; the payment is started from the project UI through the EWDS command channel (the reply carries a payment-provider hosted page), from the billing portal, or automatically when the consumer has enabled automatic payment. Twint and PayPal are out of scope.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: arbitration A1: money goes directly from consumers to producers; the platform never routes funds. Hosted payment page returned over the EWDS (A7).
