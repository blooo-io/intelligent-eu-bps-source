# REQ-015

The system shall keep each producer's connected account on a manual payout schedule and pay out the available balance to the producer's bank account when the producer requests a withdrawal (EWDS command or billing portal) or automatically if the community enables it; a withdrawal for a period is allowed once all invoices of that period where the participant is the producer are Paid, and a payout is forced before the payment provider's holding limit.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: arbitration A2: 'payout' of D5.5 scope §7.2 = payout from the producer's connected account to their bank; no platform transfer.
