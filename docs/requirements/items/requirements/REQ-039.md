# REQ-039

The system shall generate, for each invoice, a PDF produced by the billing service (not by the payment provider), issued in the name of the producer with the self-billing mention and addressed to the consumer, showing the trades covered and the amounts; store it in the billing database, make it available to both parties and to the community manager, and send it to both parties by e-mail when it is issued.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: arbitrations A4 + A6: producer is the legal issuer (self-billing); in-house PDF.
