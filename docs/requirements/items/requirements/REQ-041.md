# REQ-041

The system shall reconcile calculated balances with the corresponding payment and payout results and flag discrepancies or incomplete settlements; it shall also check, per market, that the sum and number of ingested trades match the ClearingResult (tradedQuantity, numTrades) and flag any gap.
