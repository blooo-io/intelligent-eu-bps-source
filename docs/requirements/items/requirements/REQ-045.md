# REQ-045

The system shall not issue invoices in the name of a producer, nor accept payments to them, until the producer has completed the payment provider's onboarding and KYC; a consumer must have a valid payment method registered before paying.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v5 (2026-10-07): working assumptions WA-01..WA-19 (producer-as-bank model). Based on WA-07, WA-09; to validate later (see Assumptions).
