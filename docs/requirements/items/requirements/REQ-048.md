# REQ-048

The system shall publish on the EWDS, through its Client Gateway, each issued Invoice (issuer producer, recipient consumer, tradeIds, paymentIds) and each Payment status change, on two topics created by EWAG from JSON schemas (2020-12) proposed by Blooo: billingInvoice and billingPayment, version 1.0.0, owner integration.apps.intelligent.auth.ewc, based on the INTELLIGENT Invoice and Payment schemas and extended with billingMode (live/virtual). The payment status of trades is read from these records; the system shall not change trade statuses. Payloads contain no personal data (no StripePayment, no Actor details, no legal invoice number); the grid-fee amount is 0.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v5 (2026-10-07): working assumptions WA-01..WA-19 (producer-as-bank model). Based on WA-16; to validate later (see Assumptions).
