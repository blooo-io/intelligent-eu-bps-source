# REQ-049

The system shall derive each user's role (community member, community manager, operator) and community membership from the claims of the tokens issued by the IdP, and enforce authorisations accordingly.

> [!note] Review
> INT-463 (2026-11-26): on the product EWDS path, BPS authorises commands by CGW DID allow-list + `actorId`/role (REQ-052), not by end-user IdP JWT. IdP token claims still apply to R2M sessions and to BPS ops / S2S JWT role claims (NFR-013).
