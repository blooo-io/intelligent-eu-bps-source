# REQ-051

The system shall onboard every participant (consumer, producer, prosumer) on the payment provider with a connected account whose onboarding includes the identity verification (KYC), and verify each community manager's identity through the payment provider's identity verification; it stores the verification result and its logs in the billing database and sets the kycVerified flag.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: arbitration A5: connected account for every participant, identity verification only for community managers.
