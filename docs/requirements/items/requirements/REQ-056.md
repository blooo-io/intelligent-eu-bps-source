# REQ-056

The system shall not invoice a pair whose net amount for the period is below a minimum threshold configurable per community (default 5 EUR / 5 CHF); the amount is carried forward to the next period, and an invoice is forced at the end of the calendar year or when a participant leaves the community.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: reactivated — threshold per pair kept (A3); early settlement stays removed.
