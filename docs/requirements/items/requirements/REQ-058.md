# REQ-058

The system shall maintain a participant registry linking each participant's identifier (Actor UUID) to their GSY DEX actor identifier, community and meter, maintained by the community manager, and use it to attribute every ingested trade to a buyer and a seller; trades whose parties are unknown are flagged and not invoiced.
