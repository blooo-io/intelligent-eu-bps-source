# REQ-059

The system shall produce, for each billing period, one statement per participant (ontology Billing record) showing the total bought, the total sold, the net balance, and the list of the participant's invoices with their status; the statement is informative and not a payable document.

> [!note] Review
> Spec v6.1 (2026-11-24) arbitration: two-level model, Level 1: the 'one invoice object per user per month' of D5.5 scope §7.2 is the participant statement; payable documents are the pair invoices (REQ-054).
