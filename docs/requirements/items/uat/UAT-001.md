# UAT-001 → REQ-001

**Given** settled trades of a billing period available in GSY DEX, including one trade indexed after its window was first queried and one trade returned twice,
**when** the ingestion layer sends tradesQuery requests in windows of at most 24 h, re-queries recent windows and reads the responses through the Client Gateway,
**then** every settled trade is stored exactly once (idempotent on tradeId), the late trade is captured, and each trade carries the delivery period of its Market.
