# UAT-002 → REQ-002

**Given** settled trades between a consumer and two producers in a CHF community, the consumer also selling to one of them, and the community reference EUR/CHF rate,
**when** the billing run computes the period,
**then** one amount per pair (netted) and the consumer's net balance are computed, converted to CHF at that rate, matching an independently calculated reference value, and the invoice shows the rate and its date.

## Run notes

- 2026-10-17 INT-75: S8 settlement CHF uses ECB fixture rate (INT-75).
- 2026-11-09 INT-352: Amounts are netted in exact decimals and rounded once per invoice line (BR-08); the ECB rate is kept with 4 to 6 decimals (`FxRate`, e.g. 0.9412 instead of 0.94). The rate date on the invoice follows in INT-353.
- 2026-11-09 INT-353: The rate is taken on the last TARGET business day of the period (settlement date for early and final runs, previous business day on holidays), and the Stripe invoice shows it with its date (`ECB rate 0.9412 (2026-11-30)`).
