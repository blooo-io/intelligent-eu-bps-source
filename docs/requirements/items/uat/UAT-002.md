# UAT-002 → REQ-002

**Given** settled trades between a consumer and two producers in a CHF community and an ECB EUR/CHF reference rate for the last business day of the period,
**when** the billing engine runs,
**then** one amount per consumer→producer pair is computed, converted to CHF at that rate, matching an independently calculated reference value, and the invoice shows the rate and its date.

## Run notes

- 2026-10-17 INT-75: S8 settlement CHF uses ECB fixture rate (INT-75).
- 2026-11-09 INT-352: Amounts are netted in exact decimals and rounded once per invoice line (BR-08); the ECB rate is kept with 4 to 6 decimals (`FxRate`, e.g. 0.9412 instead of 0.94). The rate date on the invoice follows in INT-353.
