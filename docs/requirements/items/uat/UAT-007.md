# UAT-007 → REQ-007

**Given** an invoice covering several trades between a consumer and a producer in a community with a configured VAT rate,
**when** either party views the invoice,
**then** each trade appears with delivery period, kWh, price and amount, and the energy amount, VAT and total are correctly summed, with no grid-fee line.

## Run notes

- 2026-11-09 INT-354: **Pass**. Each trade is a line on the public invoice and on the Stripe invoice (delivery period, kWh, price per kWh, amount). The lines plus the carried-in balance equal the energy amount; energy plus VAT equals the total. No grid-fee line (A20). Covered by settlement `buildInvoiceTradeLines`, invoicing `getPublicInvoiceForParticipant` and the invoices page.
- 2026-10-17 INT-142: InvoicesPage detail shows energy/VAT/total and states that grid fees are not on P2P invoices.
