# UAT-013 → REQ-013

**Given** a valid erasure request submitted through the portal for a participant with past invoices,
**when** the compliance stakeholder processes it,
**then** personal data is erased or anonymised in billing, erasure requests are sent to the payment provider and the identity provider, invoices under legal retention are kept, and a confirmation is produced.
