# UAT-014 → REQ-014

**Given** a consumer with an open invoice from a producer who has a verified Stripe Connect account (test mode),
**when** the consumer pays by card or SEPA Direct Debit,
**then** a payment is created on behalf of the producer's connected account, succeeds, and no funds are held on the platform account.

## Run notes

- 2026-11-09 INT-361: product decision — V1 offers **cards only**. The SetupIntent and the Payment Element no longer offer SEPA Direct Debit, because Stripe does not share a platform `sepa_debit` PaymentMethod with the producer's connected account (direct charge, INT-355). A SEPA method saved earlier is still displayed and is refused at pay time (`sepa_debit_not_shareable`). SEPA with a per-producer mandate is deferred to the next release.

Automated in `apps/web` (INT-142): Pay calls `POST /v1/invoices/{id}/payments` (AC-07 → Stripe Connect). Card/SEPA collection stays Stripe-hosted; portal asserts the payment response. Deeper Connect destination charges covered in payments module tests, 2026-10-17.

INT-355 (2026-11-09): the payment is now a **direct charge** — AC-07 clones the consumer's platform PaymentMethod to the producer Connect account and pays the producer's own Stripe invoice there (`invoices.pay`); `transfer_data.destination` is removed, so the platform never holds the funds. Covered by unit tests in `services/payments` (`stripe-psp.adapter.spec.ts`, `payments.service.spec.ts`) and `services/invoicing` with a mocked Stripe client. A manual Stripe test-mode check of the adapters (no deployment) paid a card invoice on a fresh Custom account: invoice `paid`, charge on the producer account with no `transfer_data`, no platform charge, and a repeated Pay made no second charge. **SEPA is not covered:** Stripe refuses to share a `sepa_debit` PaymentMethod with a connected account ("cannot be shared to a sub-account"), so SEPA payments are refused with `sepa_debit_not_shareable` until a per-producer mandate flow is decided. The Pass above was recorded on the earlier destination-charge flow. The status stays as it is until the card flow is re-run on `dev` (INT-251 money-path e2e, which now also looks on the producer account) and the SEPA decision is taken.

INT-276: SetupIntent advertises `allowed_payment_method_types: [card, sepa_debit]` (Stripe SDK v23). SEPA path uses Stripe test PaymentMethod `pm_sepa_debit` (IBAN DE89370400440532013000). Account must have SEPA Direct Debit enabled in Stripe test mode — otherwise Elements shows card only. Pay events carry `method: sepa_debit` when that PM is attached.
