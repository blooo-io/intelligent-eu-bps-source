# UAT-052 → REQ-041

**Given** a billing period with computed balances, a mix of completed, failed and missing payments/payouts, and one market whose ingested trades are one short of its ClearingResult.numTrades,
**when** the reconciliation runs,
**then** every balance is matched to its payment or payout result, each discrepancy or incomplete settlement is flagged, and the incomplete market is flagged with the missing quantity.
