# UAT-062 → REQ-050

**Given** a producer who has not accepted the current Terms & Conditions,
**when** a billing run is triggered or they open the portal,
**then** no invoice is issued in their name until they accept the terms including the self-billing mandate, after which the version and timestamp are recorded.

## Run notes

Automated in `apps/web` (INT-139): TermsPage loads `GET /v1/terms/current` and posts `POST /v1/me/terms-acceptance` with `selfBillingMandate`; producers without the mandate are rejected by AC-03 (covered in participants service tests). Portal records version and timestamp from the response, 2026-10-17.
